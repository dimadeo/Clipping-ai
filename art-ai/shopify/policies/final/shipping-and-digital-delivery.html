Two ways to receive your artwork

the dark matters sells physical prints made and shipped through Gelato's print-on-demand network and digital files delivered by email. Each product listing states what is included. Buying a physical print does not include its digital file unless expressly stated, and a digital purchase does not include printing, framing or shipping.

Physical prints and worldwide delivery

We offer international delivery to the destinations supported for the selected product at checkout. Availability depends on the destination, print format and fulfilment capability; some locations may be unavailable. Gelato may use production partners in different countries. We remain your seller and point of contact.

Production, delivery estimates and charges

Physical prints are produced after ordering. Available shipping methods, delivery charges and estimated production and transit times are shown before you pay. Estimates vary by product and destination. Any delivery date we expressly agree with you forms part of your order. If no different delivery period is agreed, UK consumer orders will be delivered without undue delay and within the statutory 30-day period. We will contact you about material delays and honour any applicable cancellation or refund rights.

US orders and shipment delays

For physical orders to the United States, we will ship within the timeframe stated before purchase, or within 30 days where no timeframe is stated. If we cannot do so, we will notify you, provide an updated shipment estimate and offer the choice of agreeing to the delay or cancelling for a prompt refund. We will obtain any consent required for an extended delay and refund unshipped orders where required. Production time is not an exception to these obligations. Refunds required under the US shipment-delay rule are issued within the applicable deadline, normally within seven working days; your payment provider may take additional time to display the credit.

Tracking and separate parcels

We send dispatch and tracking information by email where the service provides tracking. Items may be produced in different locations and arrive in separate parcels. Keep your checkout email and delivery address accurate. Contact hello@thedarkmatters.art promptly if a correction is needed. Production or dispatch may make a change impracticable, but this does not remove a cancellation right you otherwise have.

Taxes and customs

Taxes and shipping charges collected by us are displayed at checkout. Cross-border deliveries may involve import VAT, customs duties or carrier handling fees where these have not been collected at checkout and local law makes them payable by the recipient. Any known additional charges or the possibility of charges that cannot reasonably be calculated in advance will be disclosed before purchase. Do not assume that every order will be manufactured in your own country.

Delivery problems

Contact us if your parcel is damaged, incorrect, overdue or shown as delivered but cannot be found. We will investigate and provide the remedy required by law. For UK consumer orders using our delivery arrangements, the risk of loss or damage normally remains with us until you or someone you identify takes physical possession. We do not automatically treat a carrier scan as conclusive proof that you received the order.

Digital email delivery

Once payment is confirmed, your download link is sent to the email address provided at checkout within the delivery period stated on the product page or checkout. Where immediate supply affects a statutory cancellation right, it is subject to the required express consent and acknowledgement. Check your spam folder and contact hello@thedarkmatters.art if the email or file is missing or inaccessible. After verifying the order, we can reissue a working link at no additional charge.

File requirements and personal use

File types, included dimensions and recommended print sizes are stated on each digital product page. You need a compatible device, an internet connection and suitable software to download and open the files. Any material download limits or link-expiry terms are disclosed before purchase. Keep a secure backup after downloading. Use is governed by our Intellectual Property and Personal-Use Licence.
