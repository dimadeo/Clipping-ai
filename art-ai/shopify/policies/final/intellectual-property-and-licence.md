Unless a product expressly includes a separate written commercial licence, a digital purchase grants you a non-exclusive, personal, non-commercial licence to download, keep and print the purchased artwork for your own private use. You may display your prints in your home. The licence does not give you ownership of the underlying intellectual property or exclusive rights to the design.

## Permitted uses

You may keep the file on your personal devices and make secure backups. You may print it yourself or give it to a professional printing service solely to make prints for your own personal use. The printer must not use, retain beyond fulfilment or redistribute the file for any other purpose. You may resize or crop the file as reasonably needed to print it for that permitted use; this does not create ownership of a new commercial design.

Commercial use requires our prior written permission, including use by businesses, hospitality venues, agencies or resellers.

## Copyright and other rights

Where copyright or other intellectual property rights subsist, they remain with the dark matters, the artist or the relevant rights holder, as applicable. No copyright is assigned by a purchase. Product descriptions must accurately state the work's medium and provenance; a licence does not imply exclusive ownership or copyright protection in every element in every country.

## Physical prints

Buying a physical print gives you ownership of that physical item after payment and delivery, but does not transfer the right to reproduce the artwork. This statement does not restrict any lawful right to give away or resell the particular physical copy you own. Making new copies for resale is not authorised.

## If a purchase is cancelled or refunded

Where the digital contract is cancelled or fully refunded and the licence ends, stop using the digital file and delete your copies, except to the extent retention is required by law or expressly permitted in our written agreement. A partial refund does not automatically terminate the licence. Any action we take for breach must be proportionate and does not remove statutory consumer rights.
