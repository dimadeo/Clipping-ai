For UK consumers, the usual cancellation period for digital content is 14 days after the contract is made. For non-personalised prints, you may tell us that you wish to cancel within 14 days after delivery. If a single order is delivered in separate parts, the period runs from receipt of the last part. A standard artwork printed after you order is not treated as personalised merely because it is produced on demand.

## Refunds for standard-print cancellations

We refund the goods and, when the whole order is cancelled, the cost of our least expensive standard delivery option. Premium delivery upgrades are not refundable beyond the standard-delivery amount. We use the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, without a refund fee. We may withhold a cancellation refund until we receive the goods or evidence that you have sent them, whichever is earlier; we then refund within 14 days of that event. If we have agreed to collect the goods, we refund within 14 days of your cancellation notice. We may deduct a lawful amount for loss in value caused by handling beyond what was needed to inspect the item, where the required cancellation information was provided.

## Genuinely personalised or bespoke prints

The change-of-mind cancellation right may not apply to goods made to your individual specifications or clearly personalised, such as a print incorporating your name, photograph or bespoke design. We will identify these products before purchase. Selecting a standard size, frame or catalogue design does not automatically remove your cancellation rights. Personalisation does not remove your rights if goods are faulty, damaged or not as described.

## Damaged, defective, incorrect or missing physical orders

Under UK consumer law, faulty goods generally carry a 30-day short-term right to reject, followed by applicable repair, replacement, price-reduction or final-rejection rights. We remain responsible to you as the seller, even where Gelato or a delivery partner fulfils the order. Supplier reporting windows do not shorten your rights against us. Please contact us if a delivery is overdue or lost; you do not need to resolve a claim with the carrier yourself.

## International customers

We also offer the standard-print cancellation procedure above to US customers; it is our store policy, not a claim that US law always gives a 14-day cooling-off period for online purchases. Nothing in this policy removes mandatory rights in your state or country. If local consumer law gives additional remedies, those rights apply where required.
