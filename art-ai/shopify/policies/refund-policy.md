## Digital downloads

Digital products are supplied by an emailed download link. No physical print or frame is included unless the product listing says otherwise. Please check the stated file format, dimensions, resolution and software requirements before buying.

## Cancelling a digital purchase

For UK consumers, the usual cancellation period for digital content is 14 days after the contract is made. If you expressly request supply during that period and acknowledge that your cancellation right will end once supply begins, that right ends when supply begins, provided the required confirmation is supplied. Where those requirements have not been met, your statutory cancellation right remains. We do not offer an additional change-of-mind refund after digital supply has begun and your cancellation right has validly ended. This does not affect remedies for faulty, misdescribed or undelivered content.

## Missing, expired or faulty download links

If your email does not arrive, check your spam folder and contact us. We will verify the purchase and reissue a working link, or provide an alternative way to obtain the purchased file, without an additional charge. A reissued link carries the same personal-use licence. If a file is corrupted, incorrect or does not meet its description, we will provide the remedy required by law. This can include a corrected or replacement file and, where appropriate, a price reduction or refund. You do not lose these rights simply because you downloaded the file.

## Standard physical prints: changing your mind

For non-personalised prints, you may tell us that you wish to cancel within 14 days after delivery. If a single order is delivered in separate parts, the period runs from receipt of the last part. After notifying us, return the goods within a further 14 days. We offer this procedure for standard physical prints ordered from our website; any greater mandatory local rights still apply. A standard artwork printed after you order is not treated as personalised merely because it is produced on demand.

## Return procedure and costs

Email hello@thedarkmatters.art or send a clear cancellation statement to our business postal address. You may use the optional form below, but do not have to. We will give you the correct return instructions. Do not send returns directly to Gelato or to a printer without instructions; Gelato does not provide our customer return service. Your cancellation does not depend on our prior approval. For a change-of-mind return, you pay the direct return postage if this cost responsibility was disclosed before purchase. For items that cannot normally be returned by post, the return cost or an estimate will be disclosed before purchase. We cover reasonable return costs where goods are faulty, damaged or incorrectly supplied. Please pack prints securely. Original packaging is helpful but is not a condition of your statutory rights.

## Refunds for standard-print cancellations

We refund the goods and, when the whole order is cancelled, the cost of our least expensive standard delivery option. Premium delivery upgrades are not refundable beyond the standard-delivery amount. We use the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, without a refund fee. We may withhold a cancellation refund until we receive the goods or evidence that you have sent them, whichever is earlier; we then refund within 14 days of that event. If we have agreed to collect the goods, we refund within 14 days of your cancellation notice. We may deduct a lawful amount for loss in value caused by handling beyond what was needed to inspect the item, where the required cancellation information was provided.

## Genuinely personalised or bespoke prints

The change-of-mind cancellation right may not apply to goods made to your individual specifications or clearly personalised, such as a print incorporating your name, photograph or bespoke design. We will identify these products before purchase. Selecting a standard size, frame or catalogue design does not automatically remove your cancellation rights. Personalisation does not remove your rights if goods are faulty, damaged or not as described.

## Damaged, defective, incorrect or missing physical orders

Contact us as soon as you notice a problem, with your order number, a description and photographs where reasonably possible. Photographs help us investigate with Gelato, but do not replace your legal rights. Under UK consumer law, faulty goods generally carry a 30-day short-term right to reject, followed by applicable repair, replacement, price-reduction or final-rejection rights. We remain responsible to you as the seller, even where Gelato or a delivery partner fulfils the order. Supplier reporting windows do not shorten your rights against us. Please contact us if a delivery is overdue or lost; you do not need to resolve a claim with the carrier yourself.

## International customers

We also offer the standard-print cancellation procedure above to US customers; it is our store policy, not a claim that US law always gives a 14-day cooling-off period for online purchases. Nothing in this policy removes mandatory rights in your state or country. If local consumer law gives additional remedies, those rights apply where required.
