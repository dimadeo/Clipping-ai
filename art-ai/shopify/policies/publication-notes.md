## Details needed before publication

- Confirm whether **The Dark Matters** is the registered legal entity or a trading name. If a sole trader, disclose the proprietor’s legal name as required; do not invent a company or registration number.
- Add the business postal address, applicable customer contact details and UK nation. Add company and VAT numbers only if applicable. A business email alone is not a complete trader/controller identity. A US marketing email also needs a valid postal address.
- Choose the applicable UK legal system in the Terms. US promotion does not itself turn the business into a US company or eliminate UK obligations.
- Confirm the actual download/email app, analytics, ad pixels, marketing providers and Shopify Network Intelligence/Enhanced Services settings. Finish the highlighted privacy disclosures, retention schedule, cookie-control link and US privacy-rights implementation. Do not claim that data is never sold/shared before checking advertising and Shopify settings.
- Confirm Gelato is connected and which products, destinations, production times, shipping charges and customs disclosures checkout can actually provide. The drafts do not promise universal geographic availability or a fixed speed.
- Arrange a usable returns address and process. Gelato does not accept the shop’s change-of-mind returns. The proposed policy voluntarily gives US customers the same 14-day cancellation procedure for standard prints; this is a business commitment to accept before publication.
- Confirm the licence choice: private personal use only, no commercial use, distribution or new copies for resale. A separate commercial licence may be sold later. Confirm the rights/provenance of each work; AI-generated material is not automatically copyright-protected in the US.
- Set an effective date and retain the policy version supplied with each order. Check the contract-acceptance wording against actual Shopify order and download emails.
- Provide applicable privacy information to nominated gift recipients. Asking the purchaser to tell them does not transfer the store’s own transparency responsibility.
- For California promotion, assess CalOPPA separately from CCPA: a commercial site collecting identifiable information from California consumers can owe conspicuous privacy-policy duties even below CCPA thresholds. Complete the disclosures about third-party tracking across sites/over time, Do Not Track, how customers review or request changes to information, the effective date and how material policy changes are notified. [California Attorney General CalOPPA guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/business-privacy).

## Digital checkout wording and implementation

For immediate digital delivery to UK customers, use an **unticked, specific choice** such as:

> Please start supplying my digital artwork now. I expressly agree to supply before the end of my 14-day cancellation period and understand that I lose my right to cancel once supply begins.

This must be separate from marketing consent. Record the wording, consent, order and time, and confirm it with the contract information in a saveable email. The policy alone does not remove cancellation rights. Check express checkouts, Buy now and other routes as well as the normal cart. If valid consent and acknowledgement are not obtained, do not claim the cancellation right has ended; offer a compliant delayed-supply route or otherwise preserve the applicable right.

Product-page microcopy:

> Digital download only — no physical item. A download link will be emailed after payment within the stated delivery time. Personal use only; no redistribution or printing for resale. Cancellation and faulty-file rights are explained in our Refund Policy.

For physical prints:

> Printed to order and shipped through Gelato. Standard catalogue prints retain the return rights in our Refund Policy. Any genuine personalisation and delivery charges will be identified before payment.

## Why the refund policy is not “all sales final”

Early digital supply requires express consent and acknowledgement to end the UK cancellation right, with confirmation of those choices. Statutory remedies for faulty or misdescribed content remain. [GOV.UK digital selling rules](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/online-selling), [Trading Standards digital-content guidance](https://www.businesscompanion.info/en/quick-guides/digital/digital-content).

Producing a standard design after an order does not automatically make it bespoke. Selecting standard options is not itself enough to invoke the personalised-goods exception. [Trading Standards online-selling guidance](https://www.businesscompanion.info/node/1368/printable/print). Eligible returns need a seller-run process, even though [Gelato’s supplier policy](https://support.gelato.com/en/articles/8996072-what-is-your-return-policy-and-quality-guarantee) does not provide one. [GOV.UK returns rules](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds).

US physical orders must have supportable shipment promises and the required delay-consent/refund process. [FTC merchandise-order rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule). US commercial email requires opt-outs and a valid postal address. [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

## Privacy and intellectual-property sources

- [ICO privacy-notice guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/privacy-notices-and-cookies/create-your-own-privacy-notice/png-general-business/) — match disclosures to actual collection, purposes and retention.
- [ICO cookie-consent guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/how-do-we-manage-consent-in-practice/) — configure the controls; a notice is not a consent system.
- [ICO privacy-complaint process](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/how-to-deal-with-data-protection-complaints/) — distinguish the 30-day complaint acknowledgement from rights-request timing.
- [Shopify Consumer Privacy Policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy/consumers) and [Shopify data-processing agreement](https://www.shopify.com/legal/dpa) — Shopify’s role varies with the services and settings used.
- [Gelato privacy policy](https://www.gelato.com/legal/privacy) and [data-processing terms](https://www.gelato.com/legal/data-processing-terms) — production, logistics and cross-border processing.
- [California Attorney General CCPA guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) — applicability must be assessed; this draft is not a claim that every state statute applies or that a complete CCPA programme exists. Targeted advertising may trigger additional duties. Determine other relevant states before campaigns expand.
- [US Copyright Office AI report](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) — human authorship and ownership must be assessed; do not promise copyright in every AI-generated element.

This is a drafting package, not a certification of UK, US or worldwide legal compliance. A solicitor should review the completed business details, advertising/data setup and final checkout flow for a launch-ready legal position.
