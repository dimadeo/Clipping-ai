Launch checklist for THE DARK MATTERS — created 12 September 2026.

- [ ] **2. Create the realistic room and gallery** - after milestone 1; Codex, then owner visual approval.

  - Complete when every launch artwork has a documented file-to-size mapping and approved crops.
- [ ] **4. Prepare Shopify products and delivery** - after milestones 1 and 3; Codex.

  - Complete when all launch products are connected with correct files and no duplicate listings.
- [ ] **5. Test the complete customer journey** - after milestones 2 and 4; Codex.
  - Check mobile/desktop navigation, size switching, artwork proportions, readability and loading.
  - Verify displayed prices and selected options through checkout using an available safe test method; no real charge without permission.
  - Verify that a test customer receives and can download the correct file; confirm digital-only items do not request shipping.
  - Complete when results are recorded and launch-blocking failures are fixed.
- [ ] **6. Final approval and publication** - after milestone 5; owner approval, then Codex.
  - Present the finished preview, product list, prices, test results and any limitations.
  - Obtain explicit final go-live approval, then publish the approved storefront/products.
  - Check the public domain, product pages and download flow after launch; record a rollback path before publishing.
  - Complete only when the live site has been verified and its link recorded here.
